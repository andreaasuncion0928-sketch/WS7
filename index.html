01 — OUR STORY

THE BEGINNING

COFFEE & MEMORIES

IT STARTED WITH COFFEE.

For a long time, quiet feelings stayed silently kept inside — unspoken, unhurried, but gently growing beneath every conversation. Then, in one quiet shift of time, everything suddenly sparked into motion.

What started as quiet coffee dates quickly became long conversations, endless calls, road trips into the horizon, and countlessly shared memories that turned into our favorite rituals.

Somewhere between one coffee cup and the next, we realized we had found something worth holding on to for a lifetime.

We became each other's favorite person to call, the one we wanted beside us for ordinary, quiet days and unforgettable ones alike.

THE PROPOSAL

Tagaytay

It wasn’t crafted like a grand theatrical stage, but as a quiet, spontaneous weekend away in Tagaytay.

Surrounded by dinner, fresh air, soft flowers, and a familiar warmth in our hands, the beauty of the moment simply swept over us naturally as the sun began to dip beneath the ridge.

Without pretense, the question simply came from the heart, changing everything in a single breath.
