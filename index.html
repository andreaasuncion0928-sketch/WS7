A Love Story, One Cup at a Time

01 — THE BEGINNING

IT STARTED WITH SILENCE.

We met through mutual friends, carryings unsaid feelings that quietly lingered for years. What began in gentle silence eventually found its spark, turning unspoken thoughts into late-night calls, effortless laughter, road trips, and endless shared memories.

Somewhere between one coffee and the next, we realized we had found something truly worth holding on to. We became each other’s favorite person to call, the one we wanted beside us for both ordinary days and unforgettable ones.

02 — THE PROPOSAL

A SUDDEN SPARK IN TAGAYTAY

It wasn't a heavily scripted moment—just a quiet weekend in Tagaytay that gently unfolded on its own terms. Between sunset light, cool breezes, and our usual coffee, the universe seemed to pause.

Right there in the simplicity of the evening, the question moved between us naturally. It felt like the only possible next answer to our story. She said yes.

03 — THE NEXT CHAPTER

BEGINNING OUR NEXT CHAPTER

"What started with coffee became a life we chose together. Now, we are beginning our next chapter — and we would love to celebrate it with the people who have been part of our story."
